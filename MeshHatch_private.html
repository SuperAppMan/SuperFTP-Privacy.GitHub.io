MeshHatch privacy policy

MeshHatch is an Android 3D modeling app from m4coding. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email m4coding@qq.com.

Your projects

The app stores scenes, imported models and AI task history on your device. You choose when to export a file or share it with another app. A project backup can include your model and project data. Copies you export remain wherever you save or send them.

When you use AI

AI modeling sends your prompt, the selected object and relevant scene details to the model service you choose. Follow-up questions can include the related request and your answers. Before displaying newly generated or changed 3D shapes, MeshHatch creates simplified outlines on your device and sends them to the same model service in a separate safety check. These outlines omit object names and project identifiers. Original GLB files and textures stay on your device during generation. Your provider processes requests under its own privacy and retention policies. A gateway may route a request to the model provider that serves it. If a provider requires an API key, the app uses the key to authenticate with that service. Saved keys are protected using Android Keystore and excluded from project backups.

AI content reports

If you send an AI content report, MeshHatch sends the text you confirm, the selected reason, the app version and a random report ID to m4coding’s reporting service hosted by Cloudflare. Reports help the developer review inappropriate content and improve safeguards. Review the form before sending and remove any private information. The app does not attach your prompt, API key, project file or device identifier to the report. Reports are retained for up to 90 days for review. Cloudflare recovery history can retain deleted records for up to 30 additional days. To request deletion of a report, contact m4coding at the email address above.

Advertising

MeshHatch uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google's advertising SDK collects and shares data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This can include IP addresses used to estimate general location, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic and performance information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. Google's SDK encrypts data in transit. Where available, use the advertising privacy options in Settings to review your choices. Android also provides controls for the advertising ID.

Retention and choices

Local projects and task history persist until you remove them or clear the app's data. Use a project's Manage menu to clear its AI history, and remove saved API keys in model service settings. Delete exported copies separately. For data held by a model provider or advertising service, its own retention and deletion procedures apply.

Support

Feedback opens your email app. If you send a message, m4coding receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond. Please leave API keys and other secrets out of support messages.
