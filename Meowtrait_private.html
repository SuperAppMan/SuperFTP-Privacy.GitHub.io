Meowtrait Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Meowtrait ("the app") is developed by m4coding. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Photos and camera

Photos you take or pick are analyzed only on your device to recognize your cat's coat, eyes and markings. Photos are never uploaded to us or any third party, and are never used to train models. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded. A small thumbnail is kept on your device only if you save a cat profile.

2. No account

The app has no sign-up and no server of its own. Your cat profiles, unlock records and settings are stored on your device. Android may include them in your own Google Drive device backup; you can also export a backup file yourself.

3. Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads): device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change it in Me → Ad privacy choices.
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions): purchase records processed by Google.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: anonymous usage events (for example which screens are used and which cat traits were recognized, never photos or pet names) and crash reports. You can turn off usage statistics in Me → Share usage statistics.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

5. Deleting your data

Delete a cat in the app, or uninstall the app / clear its data in system settings to remove all local data.
