TG Media Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · App: TG Media (package com.tgmedia.app) · Developer: m4coding

This policy explains what information TG Media ("the app", "we") handles and why. TG Media is a local-first file manager for Telegram media. The core features are free; the free version shows ads, and an optional paid subscription, TG Media Pro, removes them.

1. Your files stay on your device

  • TG Media does not require a Telegram login and does not access your Telegram account or chats.
  • Files you import, save, preview or download are stored only on your device. We do not upload your media, file names, folders or search terms to any server, and we do not operate servers that receive them.
  • Files you share are sent only to the app you choose in Android's share menu.

2. Network requests made by the app

  • Public Telegram pages. When you open a public t.me link or browse the curated public channel catalog, the app requests those public web pages and media directly from Telegram over HTTPS. Telegram may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, as with any website.
  • Advertising and Google Play services, described below.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • app interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data).

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Android settings.

The ads SDK is not started for TG Media Pro subscribers, and no ads are requested while a 24-hour ad-free reward is active.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

TG Media Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; TG Media never receives or stores your payment card or billing details. The app receives a purchase record from Google Play (such as the product, purchase state and a purchase token) only to verify on your device whether Pro is active. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Other Google Play features

If you choose to rate the app, the Google Play In-App Review dialog is shown by Google Play and is subject to Google's policies.

7. Data stored on your device

The app keeps settings, your saved-library index, favorites, download tasks, ad frequency counters and your Pro status locally on your device. This data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We do not keep copies.

8. Permissions

  • Internet: to load public Telegram pages, ads and Google Play services.
  • Notifications (optional): to show download progress and completion.
  • Foreground service: to keep downloads and background audio playback running reliably.
  • Files are accessed only through folders and files you pick with Android's system file picker or drag and drop.

9. Children

TG Media is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

  • Subscribe to TG Media Pro, or watch a rewarded video, to stop ads.
  • Change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where required), and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: m4coding@qq.com

TG Media 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日 · 应用：TG Media（包名 com.tgmedia.app）· 开发者：m4coding

本政策说明 TG Media（以下称"本应用""我们"）会处理哪些信息以及原因。TG Media 是一款本地优先的 Telegram 媒体文件管理工具。基础功能免费；免费版会展示广告，可选的付费订阅 TG Media Pro 可移除广告。

1. 你的文件只保存在你的设备上

  • 本应用无需登录 Telegram，也不会访问你的 Telegram 账号或聊天内容。
  • 你导入、保存、预览或下载的文件只存储在你的设备上。我们不会把你的媒体、文件名、文件夹或搜索词上传到任何服务器，也没有接收这些数据的服务器。
  • 你分享的文件只会发送给你在 Android 分享菜单中选择的应用。

2. 本应用发出的网络请求

  • Telegram 公开页面：当你打开公开的 t.me 链接或浏览精选公开频道目录时，本应用会通过 HTTPS 直接向 Telegram 请求这些公开网页与媒体。与访问任何网站一样，Telegram 可能收到你的 IP 地址等常规连接信息。
  • 下文所述的广告与 Google Play 服务。

3. 广告（免费版）

免费版通过 Google AdMob 展示广告。为投放和衡量广告并防止作弊，Google 移动广告 SDK 可能收集以下信息并与 Google 共享：

  • 设备标识符，包括 Android 广告 ID；
  • IP 地址及据此推断的大致位置；
  • 与广告的互动（展示、点击）以及诊断信息（如崩溃和性能数据）。

Google 如何使用这些数据，请参阅Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息及 Google 隐私权政策。你可以在设备的 Android 设置中重置或删除广告 ID，或停用个性化广告。

TG Media Pro 订阅用户不会启动广告 SDK；领取 24 小时免广告奖励期间也不会请求广告。

4. 同意（欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士）

如果你位于欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，本应用会在请求广告前通过 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）征求你的同意。你可以随时在应用内设置 → 广告隐私选项中查看或修改。

5. 购买与订阅

TG Media Pro 以自动续订订阅的形式通过 Google Play 结算销售。付款由 Google 处理，本应用不会接收或保存你的银行卡或账单信息。本应用仅从 Google Play 获取购买记录（如商品、购买状态和购买凭证），并只在你的设备上用于验证 Pro 是否有效。详见 Google 隐私权政策和 Google Play 服务条款。

6. 其他 Google Play 功能

如果你选择为本应用评分，应用内评价窗口由 Google Play 提供，适用 Google 的相关政策。

7. 存储在你设备上的数据

本应用会在你的设备上本地保存设置、已保存文件的索引、收藏、下载任务、广告频次计数和 Pro 状态。清除应用数据或卸载后这些数据即被删除，我们不保留副本。

8. 权限

  • 网络：加载 Telegram 公开页面、广告和 Google Play 服务。
  • 通知（可选）：显示下载进度与完成提醒。
  • 前台服务：保证下载和后台音频播放稳定运行。
  • 本应用只访问你通过 Android 系统文件选择器或拖放提供的文件夹和文件。

9. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

10. 你的选择

  • 订阅 TG Media Pro，或观看激励视频，即可停止展示广告。
  • 在设置 → 广告隐私选项中修改广告同意（如适用），并在 Android 设置中管理广告 ID。
  • 可随时在 Google Play →"付款和订阅"中取消订阅。
  • 卸载本应用即可删除所有本地数据。

11. 政策变更

应用功能变化时我们可能更新本政策。上方生效日期为最新版本，重大变更会在应用更新说明中注明。

12. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请联系：m4coding@qq.com
