Sneaker Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Package: com.m4coding.sneakerstudio

Sneaker Studio is provided by M4Coding. This policy explains how the app handles information. Contact: m4coding@qq.com.

Designs and local storage

The app does not require an account or sign-in. Your designs, color selections, brush strokes, settings and local progress are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your designs. You can delete saved designs in the app. Exported images, PDFs and .kicks files remain in the locations you choose until you delete them.

Your device may include app data in operating-system backups according to your device and backup-provider settings. Manage these backups through your device or provider.

Camera, files and sharing

The camera is used when you choose to scan a colorway QR code. Camera images are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers. File access is used to import design files and save exports. When you choose to share a file or code, the selected receiving app handles it under that app's privacy policy.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ads and applicable consent choices. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostics and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Google SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where required, consent messages are shown before advertising requests. Available advertising privacy options can be reopened from the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Ad availability and personalization depend on your region, consent and Google settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Purchases

Optional Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. The app reads purchase and subscription status to provide paid features and stores entitlement information in protected local storage. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google handles billing information under its policies. Purchase restoration and subscription checks require a connection to Google Play.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders and grant notification permission, the app schedules local notifications. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke notification permission in Android settings.

Retention and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Removing the app does not automatically delete exported files or copies shared with others. Third-party advertising, billing and backup providers manage their own retention and deletion processes. We cannot directly delete records held by those providers.

Children

Sneaker Studio is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be shown on this page. For questions about this policy, contact m4coding@qq.com.
