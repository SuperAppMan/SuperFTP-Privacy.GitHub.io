BodySplice Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · App: BodySplice (package com.m4coding.bodysplice) · Developer: m4coding

BodySplice is an anatomy puzzle app for learning the bones of the human body. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We built the app so that your learning data never leaves your device unless you choose to move it.

1. Information we do not collect

BodySplice has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and we run no servers that receive data from the app.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Learning data: best times, stars, unfinished puzzles, quiz, labelling and daily-challenge results, your mistakes notebook with its review schedule, and daily free-use counters.
  • Settings: language, theme, sound, vibration, read-aloud, reminder time and similar preferences.

This data stays in the app's private storage. You can export it as a backup file (you choose where the file goes), import a backup, or delete it at any time in Me › Backup & data › Reset progress. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, and you can manage it in your device settings.

Bone names are read aloud with your device's own text-to-speech engine; no audio is sent by the app.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for the notification permission and schedules one local notification per day on your device. No server and no push service are involved, and you can turn it off in Me › Settings or in the system settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (and app set ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interaction data,
  • diagnostics such as crash information of the ads SDK.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Me › Settings › Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Pro users see no ads, and the ads SDK does not request ads for them.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play which Pro products your Google account owns, so that it can unlock Pro on this device. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Children

BodySplice is intended for users aged 13 and over (for example students of medicine, nursing and other health subjects). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the ads in this app, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Google.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data on our side, requests about your learning data can be handled directly in the app (export, reset) or by uninstalling it. For data processed by Google for ads or purchases, you can exercise your rights (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) through Google's tools linked above. You can always contact us for help.

8. Security

Learning data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Exported backup files are saved where you choose; keep them safe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: m4coding@qq.com

BodySplice 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · 应用：BodySplice（包名 com.m4coding.bodysplice）· 开发者：m4coding

BodySplice 是一款通过拼图学习人体骨骼的应用。本政策说明应用会处理哪些信息以及原因。除非你主动转移，否则你的学习数据不会离开你的设备。

1. 我们不收集的信息

BodySplice 没有账号，也不需要登录。我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、照片、位置或其他个人信息，也没有任何接收应用数据的服务器。

2. 只保存在你设备上的数据

  • 学习数据：最佳用时、星级、未完成的拼图、测验、标注和每日挑战的成绩、错题本及其复习计划、每日免费次数。
  • 设置：语言、主题、音效、震动、朗读、提醒时间等偏好。

这些数据保存在应用的私有存储中。你可以随时导出为备份文件（保存位置由你决定）、导入备份，或在“我的 › 备份与数据 › 重置进度”中删除。卸载应用也会删除这些数据。

如果你开启了 Android 备份，系统可能会把这些数据包含在你 Google 账号的设备备份中。该备份由 Google 按其隐私政策处理，你可以在系统设置中管理。

骨骼名称由设备自带的文字转语音引擎朗读，应用不会发送任何音频。

如果你开启每日提醒，应用会申请通知权限，并在你的设备上每天安排一条本地通知。不经过任何服务器或推送服务，你可以在“我的 › 设置”或系统设置中关闭。

3. 广告（Google AdMob）

免费版会展示由 Google AdMob 提供的广告。为了投放和衡量广告，Google 移动广告 SDK 可能会收集并处理：

  • 设备的广告 ID（以及应用组 ID）；
  • IP 地址以及据此推断的大致位置；
  • 设备和应用信息（型号、系统版本、语言、应用版本）及广告互动数据；
  • 广告 SDK 的崩溃等诊断信息。

在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，应用会在请求广告前通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意，你可以随时在“我的 › 设置 › 广告隐私设置”中修改。其他地区可在 Android 系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID，或停用个性化广告。

Google 如何使用这些信息，请参阅：Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息 以及 Google 隐私权政策。

Pro 用户不会看到广告，广告 SDK 也不会为其请求广告。

4. 购买（Google Play 结算）

订阅完全由 Google Play 处理。我们不会收到或保存你的支付信息。应用只会向 Google Play 查询你的 Google 账号拥有哪些 Pro 商品，以便在本设备上解锁 Pro。

5. 儿童

BodySplice 面向 13 岁及以上的用户（例如医学、护理等专业的学生），不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集其个人信息。

6. 你的权利

我们这边不保存你的个人数据，学习数据可以直接在应用中导出或重置，也可以卸载应用删除。由 Google 为广告或购买处理的数据，可通过上文链接的 Google 工具行使你的权利（例如 GDPR、CCPA 下的权利）。如需帮助，随时联系我们。

7. 政策变更

应用更新时我们可能会修改本政策，届时会更新上方的生效日期，重大变更会在应用的更新说明中告知。

8. 联系我们

如有疑问或请求，请发送邮件至 m4coding@qq.com。
