Modeler 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

m4coding provides Modeler 3D (com.modeler.app), a free 3D modeling app with advertisements and optional Google Play subscriptions. Contact: m4coding@qq.com.

Your projects and files

Projects, thumbnails, textures and version history are stored in the app's private device storage. Imports use files you select through the system picker. Exports and shares go where you choose. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your models.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud and comply with privacy choices. Requests are configured for non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. We request consent where required before requesting ads. Where required, Settings provides “Ad privacy choices” to review or change consent. You can also manage your advertising ID through Android settings. Premium subscribers do not receive ads while their entitlement is active; an earlier consent record may remain with the consent provider.

Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations retrieve advertising feature switches. Google processes installation identifiers and device/configuration request information to provide this service. This release does not include Firebase Analytics, does not send custom analytics events or user properties, and does not record local feature-usage statistics. This does not disable the data processing performed by advertising or configuration services.

Subscriptions

Google Play handles payments and subscription management under its own policies. The app reads product identifiers, purchase/subscription information and expiration times to enable Premium, and stores an expiring entitlement on your device. We do not receive your card details or operate a purchase verification server. You can restore purchases or manage/cancel subscriptions through the app's Premium page and Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Optional network use

If you configure WebDAV backup, projects, thumbnails and metadata (including project name, modification time and a device identifier) are sent to your chosen server. Credentials are encrypted locally with Android Keystore. Your WebDAV provider handles that data under its policies. Optional model downloads use the specified HTTPS content server, which can see request information such as your IP address. “Email us” opens your email app; nothing is sent until you send it, and we use submitted emails and attachments to respond and investigate problems.

Permissions, sharing and security

The app and its SDKs declare internet/network state, vibration, advertising ID and Android ad-services permissions, billing, wake-lock and the general foreground-service permission used by the included WorkManager dependency. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad storage access. Network services use HTTPS. We do not sell your models or personal information. Google receives data for the advertising, consent, remote configuration and purchase services described above; these services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not promise that third-party services collect no data.

Retention, deletion and choices

You can delete projects in the app or clear app storage/uninstall to remove local data; separately delete backups from your WebDAV server. This does not delete records retained by Google for its services or cancel payments. For Google's retention and deletion controls, consult the policies linked below and your Google/Android settings. Contact us to request deletion of emails or other information you have sent directly to us. We retain such correspondence only as needed to support you and meet applicable obligations.

Children and changes

The app is intended for ages 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We update this policy when practices change and publish the new effective date here.

Providers: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising, Firebase privacy.

Modeler 3D 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-01

m4coding 提供 Modeler 3D（com.modeler.app），这是一款包含广告及可选 Google Play 订阅的免费 3D 建模应用。联系邮箱：m4coding@qq.com。

作品与文件

项目、缩略图、贴图和历史版本保存在设备的应用私有存储中。导入仅使用你通过系统文件选择器选中的文件，导出和分享发往你选择的位置。卸载应用会删除应用本地数据。我们没有应用账号系统，也没有接收你模型的开发者服务器。

广告与同意

免费版使用 Google AdMob 和用户消息平台 UMP。它们可能处理 IP 地址（可推导大致位置）、设备及广告标识、广告交互、诊断信息和同意选择，用于提供及衡量广告、防欺诈和遵守隐私选择。广告请求配置为非个性化，但这不代表不处理数据。需要同意时，应用在请求广告前征求同意；需要提供隐私选项的地区，可在设置中的“广告隐私选择”查看或更改选择。你也可在 Android 设置管理广告 ID。有效会员不接收广告；此前的同意记录仍可能保留在同意服务商处。

远程配置

Firebase Remote Config 和 Firebase Installations 获取广告功能开关。Google 为此处理安装标识及设备、配置请求信息。本版本没有 Firebase Analytics，不上报自定义统计事件或用户属性，也不记录本地功能使用统计。这不代表广告或配置服务不处理数据。

订阅

Google Play 按其政策处理付款与订阅管理。应用读取商品标识、购买/订阅信息及到期时间，用于启用会员，并在设备上保存有到期时间的权益缓存。我们不接收银行卡信息，没有开发者验单服务器。可在会员页恢复购买，或前往 Google Play 管理、取消订阅。卸载应用不会取消订阅。

可选联网

如果配置 WebDAV 备份，作品、缩略图和元数据（项目名称、修改时间、设备标识）发往你选择的服务器；凭据使用 Android Keystore 在本地加密。服务商按其政策处理数据。可选模型下载访问指定 HTTPS 内容服务器，服务器能看到 IP 等请求信息。“发邮件给我们”仅打开邮件应用，你发送后才会发出内容，我们用于答复及排查问题。

权限、共享与安全

应用及 SDK 声明网络/网络状态、振动、广告 ID 和 Android 广告服务、支付、唤醒锁，以及随 WorkManager 依赖合并的通用前台服务权限，不申请位置、通讯录、相机、麦克风或整个存储空间权限。网络服务使用 HTTPS。我们不出售你的模型或个人信息。Google 会为上述广告、同意、远程配置和购买服务接收数据，适用其服务条款及隐私政策；不能理解为第三方服务不收集数据。

保留、删除与选择

可在应用内删除项目，或清除应用存储/卸载以删除本地数据；WebDAV 备份须在对应服务器另行删除。这不会删除 Google 为其服务保留的记录，也不会取消订阅。Google 的保留及删除控制见下方政策及 Google/Android 设置。你可联系邮箱请求删除直接发给我们的邮件等资料；我们仅在提供支持及履行适用义务所需期间保留。

儿童及政策变化

本应用面向 13 岁及以上用户，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。如认为儿童提供了个人信息，请联系我们。数据处理方式变更时，我们会更新本页及生效日期。

服务商政策：Google 隐私政策、Google 广告、Firebase 隐私。
